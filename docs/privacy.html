Privacy policy.

How the AirTextPad website handles email subscriptions and support requests.

Effective date: 25 November 2026

1. Information collected

If you choose to subscribe, the website asks for your email address and explicit consent to receive AirTextPad release alerts, early-access news and occasional free software offers. The subscriber service records the timestamp, email address, consent status and source (“AirTextPad Website”) in a Google Sheet managed by the AirTextPad team.

2. Optional subscription

Subscribing is optional. You can select “Download without subscribing” and download the Windows installer without providing an email address. The consent checkbox is not selected by default.

3. How information is used

Subscribed email addresses are intended to be used only for the AirTextPad updates described beside the consent checkbox. They are not needed to use the AirTextPad desktop app. Do not send sensitive or confidential information through the subscription form.

4. Service providers and storage

The subscription form is processed using Google Apps Script and Google Sheets. Those services process and store the information on behalf of the AirTextPad website operator, subject to Google's terms and privacy practices. The AirTextPad website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The AirTextPad desktop app is designed for local-network use and does not require an AirTextPad cloud account; this website policy concerns the website and email subscription, not every possible data practice of the desktop software.

5. Unsubscribe, access and deletion

You may opt out of future email updates or request access, correction or deletion of your subscription record at any time by emailing airtextpad@gmail.com with the subject “AirTextPad unsubscribe” or “AirTextPad data request”. Include the email address you used to subscribe. Until an automated unsubscribe facility is introduced, requests are handled by the support contact.

6. Retention and security

Subscription information is retained while needed to manage the opt-in list and respond to requests. Reasonable access controls should be applied to the underlying Google Sheet and Apps Script project. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The website subscription is intended for people who can lawfully provide consent to receive email updates. Do not submit another person's email address without their permission.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website and subscription process evolve. For questions, unsubscribe requests or privacy requests, contact airtextpad@gmail.com.
